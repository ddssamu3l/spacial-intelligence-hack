- Uncertainty: deep ensembles N=4 (UPEN, L2M), 3 LaMa nets on disjoint subsets (MapEx); variance best vs entropy/BALD. Helps modestly (UPEN 36.3 -> 40.8 with UCB). - Failures: prediction hurts when wrong, worst early (OccAnt corridor lock-in); SGM needs confidence-gated frontier fallback (worth 4-5 SR). Naive scene-completion baseline catastrophic (10.4 SPL vs 76.8). Perception front-end is usually the bottleneck: GT segmentation lifts PONI 73.6 -> 86.5, PEANUT 0.638 -> 0.756; L2M oracle-stop 34.3 -> 50.8.
